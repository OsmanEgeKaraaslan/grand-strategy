# Cookie and Storage Notice

Version 1.0 · Clash of Empires

## What the game stores in your browser

Clash of Empires sets **no cookies of its own** and uses **no analytics, advertising or tracking** tools.

It keeps one thing in your browser's local storage, which our sign-in provider (Supabase) needs to keep you signed in:

| Item | Purpose | How long |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `sb-…-auth-token` | Your sign-in session (the access and refresh tokens that prove you are signed in) | Until you log out, or until the session expires or is ended |
| `sb-…-auth-token-code-verifier` | Completes certain sign-in links securely (only during that sign-in) | Removed when the sign-in completes |

This storage is strictly necessary to provide the game you asked for, so the game does not ask for consent and shows no cookie banner. Logging out removes your session from the browser. You can also delete it at any time in your browser's settings; you will then simply be signed out.

## Hosting providers

The companies that deliver the website to your browser may set technical cookies of their own that are needed for security and delivery (for example to tell real visitors from automated attacks). We do not use them to track you.

## Human check

The sign-up, sign-in and account forms show a check by Cloudflare Turnstile that tells people from bots. It runs in a frame from Cloudflare and may use the browser storage it needs for that check; it is part of keeping the service secure, not tracking, and is used only on those forms.

## Changes

If the game ever starts to store anything in your browser that is not strictly necessary, this notice will be updated first and you will be asked for your consent where the law requires it.
