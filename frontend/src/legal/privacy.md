## 2. What we collect and why

**Account (handled by our authentication provider, Supabase Auth).** Your email address and a hash of your password, the time you signed up and last signed in, whether you confirmed your email, and technical sign-in records kept by Supabase (such as IP address and browser details). We use these to let you sign in, to confirm your address and to reset your password. The game server never receives your password or your email address.

**Player profile.** Your display name, your username (handle), your chosen emblem, whether your match history is public, and when the account was created. Your handle, display name, emblem and join date are visible to other players. You can change them on your profile page.

**Gameplay.** The games you create or join, your nation and the actions you take in a game (for example orders, diplomacy and turn ends), and the results of finished matches. Other players in the same game see what the game rules show them.

**Social features.** Friend requests, friendships, blocks, game invitations, notifications, and Quick Match searches (when you started and last kept a search alive).

**Legal acceptance.** Which versions of these documents you accepted, and when.

**Server records.** When something goes wrong, our server records technical information about the request (an internal request id, the address of the page requested, the time and the error). `[OPERATOR: whether the reverse proxy or hosting provider records IP addresses in access logs, and for how long]`.

We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and we do not sell personal data.
