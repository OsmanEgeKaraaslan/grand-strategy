# Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Clash of Empires

This policy explains what personal data Clash of Empires uses, why, who else handles it, how long it is kept and what you can do about it. It describes how the game actually works today; when that changes, this policy changes first and you are asked to read the new version.

## 1. Who we are

Clash of Empires is an independent browser game run by its developer ("we", "us"). For anything about your data, write to **{{CONTACT_EMAIL}}**.

## 2. What we collect and why

**Your account.** To create an account you give an email address and a password, and you choose a display name. Sign-up, sign-in, email confirmation and password reset are handled by our authentication provider, Supabase. Supabase stores your email address, a secure hash of your password (never the password itself), when you signed up and last signed in, whether you confirmed your email, and technical records of sign-ins such as IP address and browser type. The game server itself never receives your email address or your password: it only knows your account by an internal identifier.

**Your player profile.** Your display name, your username, your chosen emblem, the date you joined, and whether your match history is shown to others. Your username, display name, emblem and join date are visible to other players; your match statistics are visible on your profile, and your list of finished matches only if you leave that setting on.

**Your games.** The games you create or join, your nation, the orders you give (for example recruiting, moving armies, building, diplomacy and ending turns) and the results of finished matches. The other players in a game see what the game shows them about your nation and its actions.

**Friends and invitations.** Friend requests, friendships, blocks, invitations to games, in-game notifications, and Quick Match searches (when you started searching and when your search was last active).

**Your acceptance of these documents.** Which versions of the Terms of Service and this Privacy Policy you accepted, and when.

**Human check.** To keep automated sign-ups and attacks out, the forms for signing up, signing in, resetting or changing your password, resending the confirmation email and deleting your account show a check by Cloudflare Turnstile. It looks at technical information about your browser and connection (such as your IP address and browser characteristics) to tell people from bots, and gives our sign-in provider a one-time result. We do not receive or keep that information.

**Moderation.** If an administrator suspends your account, resets your name or closes a game, we record what was done, when, by whom and why. If your account is suspended, you are shown the reason.

**Technical records.** When something goes wrong, our server records technical information about the request: an internal request number, your player identifier, the page or action requested, the time and the error. To protect the game from abuse, the server also counts how many requests each account makes per minute; these counters exist only in memory and are not stored.

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not use analytics or tracking tools, we do not sell or rent your data, and we do not make automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects. The computer opponents in the game decide only about their own nations.

The only emails we send are the ones your account needs: confirming your email address and resetting your password. We do not send newsletters or advertising.

## 3. Why we are allowed to use your data

- **To provide the game you signed up for** (performing our agreement with you, the Terms of Service): your account, profile, games, friends, invitations and notifications.
- **To keep the game secure and working** (our legitimate interest in a safe and reliable service): sign-in records, the human check, error records, abuse protection and backups. Our interest does not override yours: these records are limited to what is needed and are not used for anything else.
- **To enforce the Terms of Service** (our legitimate interest in a fair game for everyone): moderation records.
- **To show that you accepted our documents** (our legitimate interest, and legal obligations where they apply): your acceptance records.

## 4. Who else handles your data

- **Supabase** provides sign-in and hosts the game database. Account emails (confirmation and password reset) are sent through an email delivery service on our behalf.
- **Cloudflare** runs the human check (Turnstile) on the sign-in and account forms.
- **Backblaze** stores our encrypted database backups in the EU. They cannot read them.
- **Our hosting providers** run the game server and deliver the website to your browser. Like any website, they process your IP address to deliver pages and may keep short technical logs.
- **Other players** see your public profile and your actions in shared games, as described above.

These providers act on our behalf. They may process data in countries other than yours. We do not give your data to anyone else, unless the law requires us to.

## 5. How long we keep your data

- **Your account and profile**: as long as your account exists.
- **Your games**: finished matches stay part of the history of the other players who took part. If you delete your account they stay, but no longer show your name (see below).
- **Friends, invitations and notifications**: until they are answered, removed or replaced; the game keeps at most the latest 200 notifications per player. All of it is deleted when you delete your account.
- **Acceptance records**: as long as the game is run, as evidence of what was agreed.
- **Moderation records**: as long as the game is run, so that decisions can be explained and reviewed.
- **Server logs**: rotated automatically; older entries are overwritten and not archived.
- **Backups**: encrypted copies of the game database are kept for up to 30 days and then deleted automatically, so data you changed or deleted can remain in them until then.

**Deleting your account.** You can delete your account yourself on the Account page (you will be asked for your password). When you do:

- your display name, username and emblem are replaced at once by a neutral placeholder ("Deleted#…"), your match history is hidden, and your profile can no longer be found;
- your friendships, friend requests, blocks and notifications are deleted, and your open invitations and searches are cancelled;
- finished matches remain in the other players' history under the placeholder name;
- the account cannot be used any more, and we delete your sign-in record at Supabase (your email address and password hash) without undue delay.

While you are still taking part in a game that has not finished, the account cannot be deleted, so that the other players' game does not get stuck. Finish or wait for the end of those games, or write to us and we will help.

## 6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, data protection law gives you rights over your personal data, such as the right to access it, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its use, to receive it in a portable format, and to complain to your data protection authority. You can change your profile on the profile page, change your password and delete your account on the Account page. For anything else, write to **{{CONTACT_EMAIL}}**; we may ask you to confirm that the account is yours before acting.

## 7. Browser storage

The game stores only your sign-in session in your browser; it sets no cookies of its own. See the [Cookie and Storage Notice](/legal/cookies).

## 8. Security

Passwords are stored only as secure hashes by our authentication provider. Connections to the game are encrypted, every request to the game server must carry a valid sign-in, and access to each game is limited to its players. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we will inform you where the law requires it.

## 9. Children

Clash of Empires is not intended for children. You must be at least 16 years old to create an account.

## 10. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes in a way that matters, it gets a new version number and you are asked to read it the next time you open the game.
